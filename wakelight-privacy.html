Wakelight Privacy Policy

Android app · Effective date: November 2, 2026

This policy explains what information the Android game Wakelight (package com.dogaccozturk.wakelight) collects, how it is used and the choices you have. Wakelight is made by an independent developer ("we").

Summary

  • The game has no accounts and never asks for your name, e-mail, age or any other personal information.
  • Your game progress is stored only on your device.
  • The game shows ads through Google AdMob. AdMob collects some device information to show and measure ads, as described below.

Information the game itself stores

The game saves the following in the app's local storage on your device, so you can continue where you left off:

  • your voyage in progress (your boat, deck, items and route);
  • your progress: Renown, unlocked crew and Storm Levels, achievements, past voyages and best scores, including Daily Voyage scores;
  • which cards, Curios, enemies, events and tips you have already seen;
  • your settings: language, music and sound volume, animation speed, reduce motion, text size and whether to ask before quitting a voyage.

This data is not sent to us or to anyone else. Until you choose a language in Settings, the game reads your device's language to start in English or Turkish; that too stays on your device. The Daily Voyage is worked out on your device from the date. All of this data is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear the app's storage. "Abandon this voyage" in Settings ends the voyage in progress.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game shows rewarded video ads and occasional full-screen ads. Rewarded videos are optional: you choose to watch one for a free reroll of a card reward, a free Oil Cask at a shop, or a Second Wind after a defeat. Ads are provided by Google AdMob through the Google Mobile Ads SDK. To deliver, measure and protect ads, the SDK automatically collects and shares with Google:

  • Device and advertising identifiers (such as the Android advertising ID)
  • IP address, which can indicate your approximate location
  • Information about how you interact with ads and the app (for example ad views and taps)
  • Diagnostic information (for example performance and crash data related to ads)

This data is used for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention, and is encrypted in transit. Google processes it under its own policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from apps that use its services
  • How Google uses information for advertising

We do not receive your advertising ID or IP address. We only see aggregated reports from AdMob (for example the number of ads shown).

Your choices

  • Consent and opt-out (EEA, UK, Switzerland and some US states): when required, the game shows Google's consent or opt-out message. You can change your choice at any time in the game under Settings → Privacy options.
  • Personalised ads: you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your Android settings (usually Settings → Google → Ads or Settings → Privacy → Ads). You can also manage ad personalisation at My Ad Center.
  • Rewarded ads are optional: you never have to watch one to play the game.

Permissions

The app uses the internet permission only to load ads. The Google Mobile Ads SDK also uses the advertising ID permission. The game does not use your camera, microphone, contacts, files or precise location.

Children

Wakelight is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Ads in the game are limited to content suitable for a "parental guidance" audience.

Data deletion

Because we do not collect personal information, there is nothing for us to delete on our side. To delete your game progress, uninstall the app or clear its storage. For data collected by Google for advertising, see the Google links above.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new effective date.
